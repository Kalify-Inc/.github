# Kalify Inc.

Hello and welcome to the official GitHub profile for **kalifyinc**!

We are an open-source organization dedicated to building free, high-quality, and community-driven tools. Our mission is to empower developers with the resources they need to make their work and lives easier.

We believe in the power of open collaboration and giving back to the community. All of our projects are and will always be free, as we are committed to providing valuable tools without barriers.

### ✨ Our Projects

Here are some of our featured projects that you can explore:

* [Steamfolio](https://github.com/Kalify-Inc/website-steamfolio/tree/main) -
  A simple way to building a customizable portfolio interface in the style of Steam. Using Bun, React and Vite.
* [Essentials](https://github.com/Kalify-Inc/website-essentials/tree/main) -
  A way to find utilities, extensions, and app tools for your computer, specially if you format your computer.
* [Onigiri Hardcore](https://github.com/Kalify-Inc/website-onigirihardcore/tree/main) -
  A website to share news about games, animes and entertainment.
* [FindYourPet](https://github.com/Kalify-Inc/website-findyourpet/tree/main) -
  A website to help find your pet if you lost or want to adopt one.
